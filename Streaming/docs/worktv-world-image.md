## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic wide website hero background for WorkTV, a blue-themed streaming site. Create a high-quality aerospace extraterrestrial landscape as if a friendly astronaut is standing on a different world. Wide 16:9 landscape, ideally 2048x1152. A spectacular large blue ringed planet in the upper right sky, distant smaller moon, luminous blue nebula and restrained stars, distant alien mountain silhouettes, cold mineral rock formations and subtle cyan glowing fissures across a rocky foreground. Horizon around lower third, atmospheric depth and thin blue mist. The rightmost third needs an open gently lit ledge/ground with relatively calm background behind a separately overlaid astronaut. The left half should have dark navy low-contrast sky and shadowed terrain, ample readable space for large white website headline; keep detailed bright landmarks to the upper right and far edges. Cinematic realistic environment concept art, tangible textured rock, elegant blue and cyan palette with a little violet deep in the sky, soft cool rim light, inviting wonder, not horror. Entirely environment: no people, no astronauts, no robots, no humanoid silhouettes, no spacecraft in foreground, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI. The character and all text will be added separately by the website. This must be a richly detailed scenic image, not a plain gradient.
